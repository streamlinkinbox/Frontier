SPECIES BEFORE SPECTACLE

Crab reference notebook.

One species, not a mixture of crab features.

Carcinus maenas: a broad mottled carapace, five anterolateral teeth per side, three frontal lobes, short stalked eyes, four pairs of walking legs, and a folded ventral abdomen. The posterior legs are only slightly flattened—not broad blue-crab swimming paddles.

A pincer is not two mechanical fingers.

The fixed finger is part of the propodus. Only the dactylus pivots to close the gape. A larger right crusher and smaller left cutter are represented here. The target test solves the arm to the bead and checks contact before attaching it; moving the bead away can cause a miss.

Sideways locomotion.

The walking clips use a reference-informed alternating tetrapod pattern. Four legs swing while the opposing set supports the body, with overlap at transitions. The scrolling ground is a tracking-view aid: imported clips are in-place and need 10 mm/s lateral translation at natural scale.

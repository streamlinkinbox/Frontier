How the model works. Torque is shaped around the published 700 Nm V12 peak, electric assist fills low-rpm demand, traction limits rear-wheel drive, aerodynamic drag rises with the square of speed, and the DCT shifts against the 9,250 rpm ceiling. The sound is synthesized in Web Audio from rpm, load, intake and exhaust layers — no audio file is required.

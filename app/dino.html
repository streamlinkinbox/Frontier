How dimension matching works: On load we scan the alpha channel to find the tight bounding box (no white padding). The rig is scaled so that Wtight × Htight maps 1:1 into the viewport's fit box. If you replace the image with a taller or wider dino, stride, leg length and tail auto-rescale — your walk never stretches.

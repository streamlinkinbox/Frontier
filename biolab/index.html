❧ FIELD GUIDE — WHAT'S TRYING TO STALL YOU

SNAPPER JAW

Edge predator. Glows red & rears up (telegraph), then lunges at your lane. Dodge after it commits, brake-check it, or stun it with a Cutter Pulse.

VINE GRABBER

Overhead canopy vine. Snares you dead-stopped — mash SPACE to cut free. Hitting it at full boost burns straight through.

SPORE PUFFER

Belches drifting spore clouds onto the track. Driving through chokes the engine to ~45% power and fogs your visor. Steer around the cloud.

SAP POOL

Living glue. Heavy slow + sluggish steering while you're in it. Boost partially powers through. Edges are usually clean.

BRAMBLE WALL & BLAST DOORS

Thorn barriers block half the track (some grow/shrink). Lab blast doors cycle shut and hold you at a standstill — time them or wait them out.

THE MOTHER MAW

Apex specimen in the Heart Chamber. Sweeps living tentacle walls across the whole road. Read the sweep, thread the gap, don't stop underneath her.

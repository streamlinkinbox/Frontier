A NATURAL COLOUR LANGUAGE

Frog reference notebook.

Neutral is natural.

Common frogs can be grey-brown, brown and olive-brown. This study stays within a restrained earth palette, with a pale underside, dark temporal patches and subtle limb markings.

A frog, not a toad or tree frog.

A broad, rounded snout, horizontal pupils, dorsolateral skin folds and powerful folded hindlimbs. Four front digits, five hind digits and webbing on the hind feet. No oversized adhesive toe discs.

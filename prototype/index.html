FRONTIER

Vertical slice — The Canopy

The forest above you is not scenery. Vines grow down through the canopy, aim at the space you are about to occupy, and grab your vehicle — a light tendril that yanks the wheel, a strangler anchor that drags you off-line, a snare net that takes your grip away.

Every vine can be answered: cut it with the bumper blade, flick the car hard enough to peel it off, scrape a tree to shear it, or boost until the tension breaks it. The canopy pressure rises the deeper you go.

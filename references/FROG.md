# Neutral frog study

## Subject and palette

An adult **European common frog, Rana temporaria**, used as the basis for a neutral olive-brown / taupe design. Stone-grey and muted-olive variants are preview tints, not separate species or scanned specimens.

## References

1. [AmphibiaWeb — Rana temporaria](https://amphibiaweb.org/cgi/amphib_query?where-genus=Rana&where-species=temporaria): rounded snout, horizontal pupils, webbed hind toes, grey/brown/olive colour range, temporal marking and pale ventral surface. This is the primary anatomical description.
2. [Herpetofauna — Common Frog](http://www.herpetofauna.co.uk/common_frog.htm): variable subdued colour, smooth skin, hindlimb barring, temporal patch and the distinction from a toad.
3. [Cobaltxstrike, R. temporaria in a pond side](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:R._temporaria_in_a_pond_side.jpg), 14 April 2026. **CC0 1.0**. The photograph was visually inspected and a reference copy is displayed at `public/references/frog-rana-temporaria-cobaltxstrike.jpg`. No part of it was projected onto or used as a texture on the model.

## What was authored

- An original continuous, smooth-union skin mesh for the torso, head and limb muscles.
- Low-saturation vertex pigments, dorsal markings, subtle normal/roughness detail and a pale belly.
- Dorsolateral folds, nostrils, mouth seam, tympana, horizontal pupils, four fingers per forefoot and five webbed toes per hindfoot.
- A deformation skeleton, subtle throat motion and an eyelid morph animation.

This is a **procedurally modelled design study**, not photogrammetry, biological motion capture or a verified AAA production asset. The geometry and motion remain authored approximations. The user redirected the active work from crab research to this frog; no crab HTML/model has been completed.

# new sketch), then two picked sketches joined from the Inspector (Curves card · Join) and extruded together.
# Run: SolidArcCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/TrimJoinRecovery.solidarc
#      --document …/TrimJoin.solidarc --script Scripts/TrimJoinWalkthrough.txt

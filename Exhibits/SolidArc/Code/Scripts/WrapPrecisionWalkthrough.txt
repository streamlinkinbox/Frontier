# ① A free curve on the side plane YZ (Right view) is the rail; a circle r 0.4 on the front plane XZ is the shape.
# ② Pick both, Swap rail (a lone circle is the rail by default), Wrap: Auto reads round x (the curve's plane), along y.
# ③ Right view in perspective: the near half of the loop is magnified, the far half shrunk, so it straddles the rail
#    although every point lies on the rail's surface (to 10⁻⁶ of the width, W66 ⑨). ④ The same view in parallel
#    projection (numpad 5): the loop lies on the rail. ⑤ Deselected: no poles (the dots are control points, off the
#    curve by nature).

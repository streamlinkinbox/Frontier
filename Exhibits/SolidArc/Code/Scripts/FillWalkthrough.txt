# SolidArc fill / flip / image-plane walkthrough — fresh closed drawings are unfilled (a faint tint), the Fill tool next
# to Trim previews the closed region under the pointer (the lens of two circles, each crescent) and a click fills it into
# a new sketch; F toggles fill on the focused sketch (Inspector › Filled); an unfilled closed sketch extrudes into
# zero-thickness walls (an open sheet); Alt+N flips the normals of a picked sheet; Construct › Reference › Image plane.
# Run: SolidArcCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/FillRecovery.solidarc
#      --document …/Fill.solidarc --script Exhibits/SolidArc/Code/Scripts/FillWalkthrough.txt

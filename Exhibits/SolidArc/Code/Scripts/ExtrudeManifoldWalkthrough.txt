# SolidArc extrude-manifold walkthrough — the Windows report "i want to extrude the shape i cant … in blender theres
# extrude manifold that allows to push in". A closed shape drawn on a body face extrudes with E and the drag direction
# picks the Boolean (Blender's extrude manifold): into the body cuts a pocket, out of it adds; Q / W / E / R still override,
# A returns to auto. A flush imprinted face (what an inward Union left behind) pushes in with face E as a prism of its
# outline cut out of the body when no push can move it (a cap pushed back past its own base).
# Run: SolidArcCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/ManifoldRecovery.solidarc
#      --document …/Manifold.solidarc --script Scripts/ExtrudeManifoldWalkthrough.txt
# Closed forms: cube 2³ = 8 · pentagon (−0.5, −0.5) (0.5, −0.5) (0.5, 0.2) (0, 0.5) (−0.5, 0.2) has A = 0.85, a 0.3 pocket
# removes 0.255 → 7.745 · triangle (y, z) (−0.4, 0.6) (0.4, 0.6) (0, 1.4) on the +x face has A = 0.32: a 0.4 boss adds
# 0.128 → 7.873, its cap pushed in 0.9 removes the boss and a 0.5 pocket → 7.585.

# SolidArc bevel merge walkthrough — the report "when we bevel and edges / vertices come close together they must merge
# or create new vertex". Blends whose rails meet used to refuse ("contact rail runs backwards", "consumed by the blend")
# or, just short of it, leave a sliver face. Now the modeller fuses them: rails meeting mid-face become one ridge edge
# shared by both blends, a loop closing over its face becomes one apex vertex, and the face swept to zero area is dropped.
# Dragging the size handle near such a size snaps onto it (a 9 px detent; Ctrl drags free), so the meet is exact.
# Run: SolidArcCapture --specimen --reset-files --recovery …/BevelMergeRecovery.solidarc
#      --document …/BevelMerge.solidarc --script Exhibits/SolidArc/Code/Scripts/BevelMergeWalkthrough.txt
# Closed forms (2 m cube = unit cube · 8): the top loop at 1 keeps 8 · 5/6 = 6.666666666666667 (fillet) and 8 · ⅔ =
# 5.333333333333333 (chamfer); two parallel top edges at 1 keep 4 + π = 7.141592653589793 (fillet) and 6 (chamfer).

snap grid on

# ① a 2 m cube, the four top edges picked (edge mode, Shift adds), B
key N Ctrl
frames 3
click @ConstructLauncher
frames 2
click @ConstructBlock
frames 2
move world -1.0 -1.0 0.0
frames 2
click world -1.0 -1.0 0.0
frames 2
move world 1.0 1.0 0.0
frames 2
click world 1.0 1.0 0.0
frames 2
move world 1.0 1.0 2.0
frames 2
click world 1.0 1.0 2.0
frames 3
expect volume ~ 8
key 2
frames 2
click world 0.0 -1.0 2.0
frames 2
click world 1.0 0.0 2.0 Left Shift
frames 2
click world 0.0 1.0 2.0 Left Shift
frames 2
click world -1.0 0.0 2.0 Left Shift
frames 2
expect selected-edges == 4
key B
frames 8
expect bevel-active == 1
expect bevel-merges == 2
expect bevel-merge-first ~ 1

# ② typed 0.98: just short of the meet — a thin strip of the top face is left
click @BevelSize
key A Ctrl
type 0.98
key Enter
frames 8
expect bevel-size ~ 0.98
expect bevel-refusal == 0
capture 373BevelMergeNear

# ③ a nudge of the size handle: inside the detent the size snaps to 1, the four rails close to one apex vertex
drag left @BevelHandle0 by 0 2 2
frames 10
expect bevel-merge-snaps > 0
expect bevel-size ~ 1
expect bevel-refusal == 0
expect bevel-preview-volume ~ 6.666666666666667
capture 374BevelMergeApexFillet

#    C: the chamfers meet in a pyramid apex
key C
frames 12
expect bevel-chamfer == 1
expect bevel-size ~ 1
expect bevel-refusal == 0
expect bevel-preview-volume ~ 5.333333333333333
capture 375BevelMergeApexChamfer
key Enter
frames 6
expect refused == 0
expect sound == 1
expect volume ~ 5.333333333333333

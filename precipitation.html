Wind & surface

What happens on impact?

Snow sticks, slides on steep faces, compacts by density, avalanches past its repose angle and drifts leeward. Rain splashes, runs downhill as droplets, infiltrates soil, is held by snow, then flows into puddles. Hail reflects with surface restitution/friction, rolls, settles as individual stones, merges into a granular ice layer only under budget pressure, then melts into the water field. Roofs shed snow and drip; tree canopies intercept and unload.

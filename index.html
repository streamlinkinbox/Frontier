Adjust the geology, then click Generate. Export saves all rock and terrain geometry as an OBJ file.

Choose Low-poly for coarse sculpted cliffs or Layered for the previous formation style. Facet detail controls the mountain base mesh resolution. Rock distortion changes low-poly rocks and sandstone in either mode; elongation applies to sandstone. Use the base-shape button to inspect the shaped base without the attached rock layer. Limestone, sandstone and slate change the cliff profile and rock geometry—not just the palette. The live Rock Surface controls add mapless procedural weathering. Use the magnifier for close inspection and Reset camera to return. OBJ does not carry this procedural shader.

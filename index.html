RESEARCH NOTES

Why this is not only a height map

STRATA keeps an elevation surface for fast interactive rendering, but each cell also carries water depth, suspended sediment, local hardness and a horizontal velocity vector.

01

Shallow water flow

Rain adds water. Surface pressure drives outflow across four virtual pipes, then velocity advects water and sediment in X/Y.

02

Pickup + deposit

Transport capacity follows slope and flow speed. Soil is picked up when capacity is available, and deposited when the carrier saturates.

03

Talus settle

A separate thermal pass moves material when a local slope exceeds the talus angle, preventing channels from becoming only vertical displacement.

04

Layer-ready output

The graph exposes soil, sediment and water as separate channels so the next step can become true layered or voxel terrain.

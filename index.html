THE ATLANTIC WALL

06 JUNE 1944. You are driving a commandeered sedan off Sword Beach. The tide is coming in behind you. Ahead: mines, concertina wire, machine-gun bunkers, and a bomber screen. The Atlantic Wall is 1.6 kilometres inland. Reach it. Do not stop.

  • Machine-gun nests in bunkers will stitch the open ground.
  • Stay on the track — the verges are mined. Tank mines are marked with an X.
  • Use earthworks as cover. Planes will bomb the vehicle.
  • If the tide catches you, the assault is over.
